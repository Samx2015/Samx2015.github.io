# XTimers 3.5.0 — copy for coverage

Prepared September 28, 2026. Developer: Xintech LLC.

## One sentence

XTimers brings count-up timers, countdowns, world clocks and time reports into one flexible Mac workspace.

## Short description

XTimers is a Mac app for keeping several kinds of time in view. Run count-up timers and countdowns, organize reusable task sets, keep world clocks nearby and review where your time went. Core timers and local reports work offline without an account.

## Longer description

XTimers brings count-up timers, countdowns, world clocks and time reports into one Mac workspace. Named tasks and reusable task sets help people move between work, study and everyday routines. A timer reading in the menu bar keeps the current task close while other apps are in use. Local reports help users look back at the time they recorded, and the workspace can be personalized through its layout and appearance. Core timers and local reports work offline without an account.

XTimers 3.5.0 includes World Calendar with week, month and year views, regional holidays and countdowns created from dates. It also provides focused timer views, report charts and flexible sound controls. Calendar data needs an initial internet download; personal Apple Calendar events are an optional, read-only view.
