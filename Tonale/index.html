Tonale

Your music, playlists, and queues in one Mac app.

Download for Mac

Version 0.1.0 · Requires macOS 14 or later · Signed and notarized by Apple · Updates itself when new versions are released.

Your own files

Add files or folders. Tonale plays the audio and video formats macOS supports and never copies, moves, or changes your files.

Apple Music

Play your Apple Music library next to your own files. Apple Music playback needs an active Apple Music subscription.

Videos from the web

Watch YouTube videos in the official embedded player and put them in the same playlists as everything else.

One library, one queue

Build playlists across all your sources. Sign in with a Xin Account to keep them on every Mac, or use Tonale as a guest.

Installing

Open the downloaded disk image and drag Tonale to Applications. Keep one copy of Tonale in Applications, and quit Tonale before you replace it. If you use XHelpers Pro, its Tonale menu opens the Tonale you installed.
