# XTimers for Mac — 3.5.0 demo

This silent, captioned demonstration shows XTimers for Mac 3.5.0, recorded in the installed app with sample tasks and edited for time. The 30-hour report covers existing synthetic sample sessions from September 21–25, 2026; those hours were not created during the short Planning run in this video.

| 0:10–0:17 | Keep time in sight. Follow Planning from the Mac menu bar. | The native Mac menu-bar item shows Planning’s live reading advancing. |
| 0:17–0:28.5 | Know where time went. Daily totals and task shares for a sample 30-hour week. | Reports shows five selected sample tasks, the September 21–25 period, 30 total hours, daily totals, and the share of time spent on each task. |
| 0:28.5–0:40 | See when work happened. Review the sessions behind the same sample week. | The Sessions tab shows the same period and tasks as a timeline of recorded work sessions. |

There is no narration, music, system sound, microphone recording, or audio track. All visible app controls, text, timer readings and charts come from native recordings; the surrounding headlines and captions are editorial text.
