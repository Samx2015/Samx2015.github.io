Install and activate on your Mac

  1. 1 Download XHelpers Pro — one app for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 13.0 or later), Developer signed and Apple notarized.
  2. 2 Open the DMG, drag XHelpers Pro to Applications, and launch it. It appears in the Mac menu bar.
  3. 3 Click the XHelpers Pro menu-bar icon, choose Activate License…, paste the license key from your email, and click Activate.
