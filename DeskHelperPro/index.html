DeskHelper Pro

A quiet menu-bar app for MacBook display control, keyboard backlight, Auto Wi-Fi Switch, Finder context menu actions, local Safes, and Secure Pocket.

Blackout Keep the built-in MacBook display dark while mirroring to an external monitor.

What is it?

Everything it does.

  • Auto Blackout Keeps the built-in MacBook display dark while it mirrors an external display.
  • Display controls Switch mirroring, dim displays until key press, sleep the Mac, and adjust display brightness or contrast from the menu bar.
  • Keyboard backlight Adjust the built-in MacBook keyboard brightness with a slider.
  • Auto Wi-Fi Switch When enabled, watches saved Wi-Fi networks and switches to a better known access point when your current signal drops.
  • Finder context menus Use New File, New Folder, Paste, Copy Path, and Open in TextEdit, Terminal, Codex, Claude, VSCode, or another compatible app from Finder.
  • Safes Encrypt selected files or folders into a single local .safe file, add to an existing Safe, or decrypt a Safe back into a folder.
  • Secure Pocket Keep small private notes, clipboard snippets, and files in a local encrypted vault, with .mhpocket package export and import.
  • 100% confidential Unlike Google Drive, OneDrive, GitHub, and other general-purpose cloud or code services, plaintext, passwords, and encryption keys stay on your Mac.
  • Privacy basics No account, no analytics, no Wi-Fi passwords, no Safe password logging, no Dock window, and clear guidance for optional Full Disk Access.
What's good about it?

Less fuss at your desk.

  • Only blacks out when it should dark when mirrored, normal when the MacBook screen is part of your workspace
  • Fast everyday actions switch mirroring, dim until key press, sleep, adjust display and keyboard brightness, open Finder paths, encrypt Safes, use Secure Pocket, and let Wi-Fi switch when needed
  • Helpful, not noisy runs from the menu bar without a Dock window, account, or analytics
For desk setups made for MacBooks that spend real time connected to an external display
