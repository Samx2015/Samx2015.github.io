XTimers Phone Verification and Reminder Consent

This page describes the XTimers phone verification and reminder consent flow. XTimers is operated by Xintech LLC. Requested verification messages and automated reminders are limited to the signed-in user's own phone contact. XTimers does not send marketing messages or offer two-way SMS chat. The separate phone and reminder controls require a compatible app and available service; the app reports channel availability before a reminder permission can be enabled.

Verify Your Phone

  1. Sign in to XTimers with an email-backed Xin Account.
  2. Open the account's Phone and Reminders settings.
  3. Enter your own phone number and request a verification code.
  4. Enter the one-time code sent to that number.
  5. If you are changing your contact, confirm the verified replacement.

Verification proves control of the number for this XTimers contact. It does not enable SMS reminders or reminder calls, create an account without an email, or enable phone sign-in. Selecting the number already verified for this contact does not send another code or change its permissions. Starting a replacement, canceling verification, or canceling a requested sign-in check keeps the current contact and its existing permissions.

Enable SMS Reminders Separately

  1. After verification, choose the SMS reminder permission.
  2. Read the disclosure and the linked SMS Terms and Privacy Policy.
  3. Affirmatively enable SMS reminders using a control that is not pre-selected.
  4. Schedule SMS reminders for yourself at that verified contact.

The English disclosure for policy version sms-reminders-2026-09-13 is:

I agree to receive automated SMS reminder messages from XTimers by Xintech LLC for reminders I schedule for myself at this phone number. Message frequency varies. Standard message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop phone reminders and HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. XTimers does not send marketing messages.

The app may display a localized version and records the exact wording accepted. The same screen provides the SMS Terms and Privacy Policy. A valid existing SMS reminder grant for the same contact is preserved when these controls become available. SMS consent never grants permission for reminder calls.

Enable Reminder Calls Separately

Reminder calls have a separate permission that is not pre-selected. They are available only for supported personal numbers in the USA and Canada, when the call service is available. Calls originate from +1 (888) 885-6365. The call disclosure explains the automated voice, the press-1 step before reading your reminder, provider processing of reminder text, and the press-9 opt-out. See the Phone Reminder Terms for the complete disclosure and delivery controls.

Consent Evidence and Changes

XTimers records consent independently for SMS reminders and reminder calls, including the contact, purpose, exact displayed disclosure, policy version, terms and privacy links, timestamp, and app source. Verification records are separate from reminder grants. Turning one channel off in the app leaves the other channel's permission unchanged. Removing or replacing the verified contact revokes both and invalidates reminders tied to the old contact. Restoring a number or permission never revives canceled or missed reminders; schedule a new reminder deliberately.

External Opt-Out and Recovery

Reply STOP to an XTimers SMS, or press 9 during a reminder call, to stop both automated phone reminder channels. A provider SMS block can also prevent requested verification codes. Replacing or removing a contact does not erase that block. For the toll-free SMS number, START or UNSTOP requests removal of the provider SMS block; it does not grant either reminder permission or restart old reminders. After availability is restored, review each permission in the app. Reply HELP or visit XTimers Support for help.
