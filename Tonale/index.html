What you can do.

Play everything

  • Files on your Mac. Add a folder or drop files in. Tonale plays every audio and video format macOS supports, right where the files are.
  • Apple Music. Your library and playlists, next to your own files. Needs an Apple Music subscription.
  • YouTube. Public videos and playlists in YouTube’s official player. No Google sign-in.
  • Audio effects. Bass, treble, width, punch and loudness compensation for your own files.

Keep it all in order

  • Mix every source in one playlist, and play it as one queue.
  • Saved filters update themselves as your library changes.
  • Find Missing Files reconnects moved files by their content, even renamed ones.
  • Find Duplicates merges items with the same content. Your files stay untouched.

Your library on every Mac

  • Sign in, or don’t. Tonale works fully without an account. A Xin Account, with Apple, Google or email, keeps your Macs in sync.
  • Only your lists sync. Playlists and saved filters travel. Your files, file paths and passwords never do.
  • No ads. No analytics. Delete your data from inside the app at any time.
