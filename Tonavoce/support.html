Tonavoce

On-device dictation for iPhone. Speak into any app; the speech model runs on your iPhone.

Setting up

  • Open Tonavoce and download the speech model (about 632 MB, once).
  • Allow the microphone.
  • In Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards, tap Add New Keyboard… and choose Tonavoce, then open Tonavoce in that list and turn on Allow Full Access.

Dictating

  • In any app, switch to the Tonavoce keyboard with the globe key and tap the mic.
  • The first time in a while, the keyboard opens Tonavoce to start the microphone. Tap ◀ at the top left to go back; the recording continues.
  • Tap Stop when you finish. The text appears at the cursor. If you moved to another field in the meantime, it waits as an "Insert" button instead.
  • The session ends on its own after a few minutes without dictation. You can change how long in Tonavoce.

Questions

  • Why does the keyboard need Full Access? iOS gives keyboards no microphone, so the Tonavoce app listens and passes the text to its keyboard. That needs Full Access. Nothing is sent over the network.
  • Why is the orange indicator on? A session keeps the microphone ready so dictation starts instantly. Sound is kept only while you dictate. Stop the session in Tonavoce to turn it off at once.
  • Which languages? 25 European languages, detected automatically.
  • The keyboard doesn't appear in a field. Password fields, phone-number pads and some apps always use Apple's keyboard.
