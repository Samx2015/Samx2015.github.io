How it works

From request to result

Your automation asks. You decide. It reports back. Every step stays in one Inbox item, so you always know whether a request was answered and how it turned out.

  1. Your publisher asks

    An app, agent, script, or server posts an alert or a request to one of your private topics.

  2. You review

    See the message, who sent it, and when the request expires, on your iPhone or Mac.

  3. You answer

    Choose from two to five options, confirm, or decline. Consequential answers ask you to confirm once more.

  4. It reports back

    Your publisher receives the decision, does its work, and reports Completed or Failed with a summary.

Developers

Send an alert with one request

Create a topic in the app and copy its private publishing URL. Anything that can make an HTTPS request can send to it: a cron job, a CI pipeline, a home server, or an AI agent.

Terminal 
curl --fail-with-body \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --data-raw '{"title":"Backups","body":"Backup completed","source":"nas-1"}' \
  https://dataol.net/x/your-private-topic
  • JSON in, notification out. body is required; title and source are optional and shown as sent.
  • Choose the attention level with attention.mode, attention.sound, and escalation, within the limits each topic and publisher allows.
  • Ask for a decision. Choices and confirmations use the publisher API with a scoped credential you create in the app. Your sender receives the decision, confirms receipt, does the work, and reports the outcome.
  • Keep the URL secret. Anyone who has it can publish to that topic. If it leaks, delete the topic and create a new one.

Questions

Good to know

How much does XActions cost?

XActions 1.0 is free, with no in-app purchases and no subscription.

Do I need a Mac?

No. Everything works on iPhone. The Mac app is optional and uses the same account, so your Inbox and topics follow you.

Do I need an account?

Not on iPhone: you can start anonymously. A free account lets you recover your data, sign in on more devices, and use the Mac app, which always requires one.

Does XActions run my commands?

No. XActions carries the request, your answer, and the result. Your own publisher receives the decision and performs the operation, so you stay in control of what actually runs.

Can I rely on it for emergencies?

No. XActions is a best-effort automation service, not an emergency, medical, life-safety, alarm-monitoring, or 911 replacement. Delivery and sound also depend on your device settings.
