XTimers by Xintech LLC SMS Terms

XTimers is operated by Xintech LLC. After a signed-in user gives explicit consent, XTimers may attempt to send a setup verification code to the user-entered proposed account phone number. After successful verification and opt-in, XTimers may attempt user-created timer/reminder SMS only to that same phone number and only when the user schedules them for themselves. XTimers does not send marketing SMS and does not allow users to send SMS to arbitrary third-party recipients. XTimers SMS is not a two-way chat or conversation service; inbound keywords are used only for STOP, HELP, START, and YES compliance handling.

Opt-In

Users opt in inside the XTimers app by entering their phone number and affirmatively checking a box that is not pre-selected. The checkbox says: "I agree to receive SMS verification codes and reminder messages from XTimers by Xintech LLC that I schedule for myself at this phone number." The opt-in screen also displays these SMS Terms, the Privacy Policy, standard message and data rate disclosure, STOP and HELP instructions, message frequency information, and no-marketing language. Consent is not a condition of purchase. See the opt-in evidence page at xintechllc.com/FlexibleTimers/sms-opt-in.html.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies. After explicit consent, XTimers may attempt to send one setup verification message when a user requests verification of the proposed account phone number. XTimers may attempt reminder SMS only after successful verification and opt-in, and only when the account owner creates and schedules that SMS reminder for themselves. Users cannot send chat replies or user-authored messages to XTimers by SMS.

Fees

Standard message and data rates may apply. Message and data rates are charged by your mobile carrier and are your responsibility.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages from XTimers. After you send STOP, XTimers will stop sending SMS messages to that phone number unless you opt in again.

Help

Reply HELP for help. You can also contact XTimers support at xintechllc.com/XTimers/support.html.

SMS Keyword Instructions

STOP opts the phone number out of XTimers SMS. HELP requests help and the support page above provides another contact path.

START or YES may opt a previously opted-out phone number back in after the user has already provided app consent for that account phone. Carrier and provider handling may vary; XTimers does not promise a particular automated response message for any keyword.
