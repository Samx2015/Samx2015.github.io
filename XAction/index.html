Send an alert with one request

Create a topic in the app, copy its private URL, and post JSON to it from anything that speaks HTTPS.

Terminal 
curl --fail-with-body \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --data-raw '{"title":"Backups","body":"Backup completed","source":"nas-1"}' \
  https://dataol.net/x/your-private-topic
  • body is required; attention and escalation pick the level, within each publisher's limits.
  • Choices and confirmations use the publisher API with a scoped credential from the app.
  • Keep the URL secret: anyone who has it can publish to that topic.

Questions

How much does it cost?

XActions 1.0 is free, with no in-app purchases or subscription.

Do I need an account or a Mac?

Neither. Start on iPhone anonymously. The Mac app is optional and needs a free account.

Does XActions run my commands?

No. It carries the request, your answer, and the result. Your own publisher does the work.

Can I rely on it for emergencies?

No. XActions is a best-effort automation service, not an emergency, medical, life-safety, alarm-monitoring, or 911 replacement.
