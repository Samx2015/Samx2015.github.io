Contact

For support, privacy questions, bugs, or ideas, email admin@xintechllc.com.

You can also use Report a Bug, Suggest a Feature, or Send Feedback from the DeskHelper menu. These items open an email draft so you can review everything before sending it.

When Reporting an Issue

When reporting an issue, please include:

  • What happened and what you expected to happen.
  • Your macOS version and Mac model, if known.
  • Whether the MacBook screen was mirrored or extended.
  • Whether the issue involves display controls, Finder context menus, or Safes.
  • The steps that reproduce the issue.

Bug report drafts may include a short diagnostic note. Remove anything you do not want to send.

Notes

  • DeskHelper appears in the macOS menu bar and does not show a Dock window.
  • Finder context menus require the bundled Finder extension to be enabled in macOS settings.
  • New File, New Folder, Paste, Open, and Safe actions may ask for folder access when macOS requires it.
  • Safe archives use the .safe extension, and Safe passwords are not stored by DeskHelper.
  • No DeskHelper account, analytics, ads, Safe password logging, or background telemetry are included.
  • Read the DeskHelper Privacy Policy for details.
