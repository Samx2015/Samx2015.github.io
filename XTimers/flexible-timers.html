01Timers and countdowns

Make time for what matters.

Track time spent, count down for a duration or keep a deadline in sight. Run one task or several, pause and resume, and keep notes with your sessions. Your timers adapt as your day changes.

Mac · iPhone · iPad

02Task sets and reusable templates

A place for every routine.

Give work, learning and everyday life their own named task sets. Save a task or a whole set as a template, then bring a familiar routine into your next session.

Mac · iPhone · iPad

03Flexible timer views

The right view for right now.

See the whole picture or give one task your attention. Mac offers List, Grid, Single and Minimal views. On iPhone and iPad, choose List, Focus or Single to suit the moment.

Mac · iPhone · iPad

In Single and Minimal views, controls fade when the pointer leaves and reappear on hover.

04World clocks

Keep your places close.

Add the cities and time zones that matter to you. Keep colleagues, family and travel plans in view alongside your timers, with the current local time for each place.

Mac · iPhone · iPad

05World Calendar

Put your day in context.

Explore holidays, cultural dates, global events and moments in history. Add a read-only view of your Apple calendars, then turn a date or event into a countdown or personal reminder.

Mac

Download the catalog once to browse it offline. Access to your Apple calendars is optional and read-only.

06Email, SMS and call reminders

A reminder in your own words.

Schedule a message to yourself for a duration or a particular date and time. Choose email, SMS or an eligible one-time call, and keep its delivery status alongside your tasks.

Mac · iPhone · iPad

Account and service availability apply. SMS and calls need your own eligible, verified number and separate consent. Calls support USA/Canada numbers, use English and do not automatically redial.

07Sound Library

Find your finishing note.

Browse 66 bundled sounds, listen before choosing and save your favorites. Import your own audio, or use interval beeps to stay aware of time while your session continues.

Mac · iPhone · iPad

08Time reports and sharing

See where your time goes.

Review sessions, daily totals or current readings. Explore charts, compare periods and filter by task or set. Keep a CSV, HTML or PDF, email a report to yourself, or share it with an expiring link.

Mac · iPhone · iPad

Local reports and exports work offline. Email and online sharing need a connection.

09Archives

Keep the work you have finished.

Archive a task set and keep its history within reach. Search past work, browse saved snapshots and open a report when you want to revisit a project or routine.

Mac · iPhone · iPad

10Appearance customization

Make the space feel like yours.

Choose light or dark, a solid color or a photo. Build a background library with your own images and make your timer workspace as quiet or colorful as you like. Mac can also rotate photos from a folder.

Mac · iPhone · iPad

Personalize the device you are using, including Mac-specific appearance overrides.

11Menu-bar and desktop integration

Keep time close to your work.

See the task name and timer reading right in your Mac’s menu bar, without opening the main window. Click for quick access to start and pause tasks, switch sets and open reports. A global shortcut and stay-on-top windows keep the controls close.

Mac

12Live Activities

Your time, at a glance.

Keep an eligible timer visible on your iPhone Lock Screen or Dynamic Island. See the task and its reading, then pause or resume supported timers without opening the full workspace.

iPhone

Requires a supported device and system permission. World clocks do not create Live Activities.

13Siri and Shortcuts

Begin with a word or a tap.

Start, pause or toggle an eligible timer with Siri and Shortcuts. Add system controls for starting a fresh stopwatch or pausing and resuming the current task.

iPhone · iPad

Standalone system controls open XTimers. Live Activity controls can act in place.

14Offline use without an account

Make a start. No sign-in needed.

Use core timers, countdowns, task sets, notes, world clocks and local reports on your device. Keep going offline and add an account whenever you want connected features.

Mac · iPhone · iPad

15Cross-device synchronization

Pick up with a familiar workspace.

Bring supported tasks, history, templates, settings and custom assets across Mac, iPhone and iPad. Your local work stays available through temporary connection problems.

Mac · iPhone · iPad

Sign in with Apple, Google, an email code or a password. Some device settings and local-only data remain on their own device.

16Protected Sync

Choose protection before upload.

Encrypt new timer content and custom uploads on your device before account synchronization. Recover your key through iCloud Keychain or a saved recovery code.

Mac · iPhone · iPad

Optional on supported accounts. Excludes reminder messages, account/sync metadata, content created before activation and local device storage.

17Multilingual support

At home in your language.

Choose from 45 language options, follow your device or select your preference. Regional dates, times and numbers, along with right-to-left layouts, help the workspace feel familiar.

Mac · iPhone · iPad

18Automatic app activity tracking

See how your app time adds up.

Opt in to track time spent in frontmost Mac apps. Review your activities by day, week, month or year, with choices for how away time is counted.

Mac · selected profiles

Available in local and legacy profiles; hidden for local-first sync accounts. App identity and idle time stay on this Mac. Websites, browsing history and window titles are not captured.

19Classic interface

Another way to make time.

Choose an English-only, local timer interface with its own separate workspace. Switch interfaces from XTimers and keep the presentation that feels familiar to you.

Mac · local only

Requires the bundled Classic runtime. Account synchronization and email/SMS delivery belong to the main XTimers interface.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed for core local timers, countdowns, world clocks and local reports. Sign in when you want account synchronization or account-based reminders.

XTimers uses a Xin Account to identify and authenticate a user. A Xin Account is the shared identity layer for XTimers and any other connected products listed in the account controls; it is not an XTimers product account and does not contain XTimers timers, reports, sounds, or SMS data.

Can I use XTimers offline?

Core timing and local reports work offline. Synchronization, online sharing and server reminders need a connection. World Calendar on Mac needs an initial catalog download; its installed catalog can then be used offline.

Can several timers run together?

Yes. Choose whether moving to another task pauses the previous eligible timer or lets it keep running. Task sets help keep different activities organized.

Are the features the same on every device?

The core workspace includes timers, countdowns, clocks, task sets and reports. Mac adds menu-bar controls and World Calendar. iPhone supports Live Activities on compatible devices, while iPad makes room for larger task layouts.

How do phone reminders work?

Reminders go to your own eligible, verified number with separate SMS or call consent. Availability depends on your account, region and the service. A scheduled call needs server confirmation; Call Me uses English and does not automatically redial.
