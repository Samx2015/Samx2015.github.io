Your Verified Contact and Independent Permissions

XTimers uses one verified phone contact for your phone reminders. You explicitly request an SMS verification code to establish control of that number. Requesting or completing verification does not grant permission for automated SMS reminders or calls. Each reminder channel has its own permission control, which is not pre-selected, and its own disclosure. Enabling SMS reminders does not enable calls, and enabling calls does not enable SMS reminders. A valid existing SMS grant for the same verified contact remains an SMS grant only.

Call Destinations and Caller Number

Reminder calls are limited to supported personal phone numbers in the USA and Canada that you own and can verify by SMS. A +1 prefix alone does not establish eligibility. XTimers checks destination support and may decline unsupported or unavailable destinations. Calls originate only from +1 (888) 885-6365. SMS destination support is determined separately; the call region restriction does not expand or narrow SMS availability.

What Happens During a Call

A call first identifies XTimers and asks you to press 1 before it reads your reminder. The initial greeting does not contain your private reminder text. If nobody presses 1, XTimers does not read the reminder into voicemail. Pressing a key is a privacy step, not identity authentication: anyone who answers your phone and presses 1 can hear the reminder.

After the reminder, press 1 to acknowledge it, press 2 for one repeat within the same call, or press 9 to stop automated phone reminders. Pressing 9 is also available before the reminder is read. XTimers does not record or transcribe your call audio. The call provider processes your phone number and reminder text to place the call and generate the automated voice, and returns delivery and keypad-action metadata.

One Attempt and Timely Delivery

Each scheduled reminder occurrence allows at most one outbound call attempt. XTimers does not automatically redial a busy or unanswered call or use SMS as an automatic fallback. A call that cannot start within two minutes of its scheduled time expires as missed. Enabling calls later, restoring service, or reconnecting a phone does not replay expired, canceled, or previously attempted occurrences. If you want another attempt, deliberately schedule a new reminder.

Calls depend on service, network, carrier, and destination availability. A call already submitted to the provider may still arrive after a reminder is canceled, a permission is revoked, or a phone is removed. XTimers applies later changes to any remaining call interaction where possible; it cannot guarantee recall of an attempt already in progress.

Change or Remove Your Phone

Turning off one reminder permission in the app revokes that channel only; the verified contact and your other permission remain. Starting a phone replacement keeps the existing contact and permissions until the new number is successfully verified and the replacement is confirmed. Canceling verification or a requested sign-in check does not remove the working contact. Selecting the same verified number leaves it unchanged.

Removing or replacing the contact revokes both reminder permissions and invalidates pending reminders tied to the previous contact. Re-adding the same number requires verification and new reminder grants. Re-enabling a permission does not restore old reminders. Contact changes do not erase provider suppression or the retained record of prior consent and opt-out.

Stop Both Automated Phone Reminder Channels

Press 9 during an XTimers reminder call, or reply STOP to an XTimers SMS, to stop both automated calls and SMS reminders for the contact. This external opt-out applies to both channels even though the app's channel controls are independent. SMS STOP may also cause the provider to block requested verification codes. Removing or re-adding the phone does not bypass the block.

For the toll-free SMS number, reply START or UNSTOP to request removal of a provider SMS block. A provider confirmation does not grant XTimers reminder permission. Review and explicitly enable each desired channel in the app after availability is restored. Old reminders remain canceled or missed. Reply HELP for SMS help or visit XTimers Support if availability remains blocked.
