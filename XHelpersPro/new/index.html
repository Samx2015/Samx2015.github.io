Display & Windows

Run the visual side of your desk without opening System Settings or a second window manager.

  • Black out the built-in MacBook display while it mirrors an external monitor; dim displays until a key press.
  • Brightness, contrast and keyboard-backlight sliders right in the menu, plus sleep, restart, shut down and macOS theme.
  • Install HiDPI presets and custom resolutions, with a keep-or-revert safety timer after every switch.
  • Snap & Arrange Windows to halves, quadrants you can customize, maximize or center, or the next display; save and restore window positions.
  • Highlight the active window with a click-through border; Keep Mac Awake for 30 minutes, hours, on power, or while an app runs.
Global keyboard shortcuts cover arrangement, display moves and app focus, with built-in Rectangle conflict resolution.

Finder, Safes & Archives

Make Finder do more, and keep selected files, passwords and encrypted Safes on your Mac.

  • New file or folder, copy path, paste or move, and open in Terminal, iTerm, VS Code, Cursor, Zed, Xcode, Codex, Claude and about thirty more, or any app you add.
  • Compress / Encrypt a selection into a local Safe, 7z, ZIP or DMG from one dialog, with or without a password.
  • Add files to an existing Safe, or decrypt only the items you choose.
  • Extract Archive opens zip, 7z, tar, gz, bz2, xz and rar, including password-protected ones, and stops before very large ones.
  • Quick Look renders Markdown right in Finder; Uninstall & Clean Up starts from the context menu.
Finder and Safe actions run only after you choose an XHelpers Pro context-menu action; Full Disk Access is optional and explained.

Pointer & Keyboard

Tune every mouse, trackpad and keyboard on the desk separately, and keep the rules on this Mac.

  • Pointer sensitivity and acceleration per device, with acceleration off when you want raw movement.
  • Scroll speed, scroll acceleration and reverse direction per pointing device, LinearMouse-compatible.
  • Remap keys and mouse buttons to keys or shortcuts, make dual-role keys, or add a Hyper key.
  • One Global Shortcuts window for display, window and utility hotkeys.
Profiles follow the device, so a travel mouse and a desk mouse each keep their own feel.

Audio, Music & Subtitles

Control what each app sounds like, keep your music in one library, and read what any app is saying.

  • Audio Mixer: per-app volume and mute in the menu, boost quiet apps up to 200%, and per-app Audio Effects (bass, treble, width, punch, loudness, spatial).
  • Music & Playlists: your Apple Music library plus local audio and video folders in one library, with playlists and saved filters that span sources.
  • Audio Player with a shared queue, Quick Video with speed, loop and always-on-top, and Extract Audio from video files.
  • Live Subtitles: on-device transcription and translation of one app's audio, with a captions-only mode and per-speaker colors (macOS 26.4 or later).
Audio Mixer and Live Subtitles use macOS System Audio Recording (macOS 14.4 or later). Audio, transcripts and translations are processed on this Mac and never saved or sent.

Screen, Text & Files

Anything on screen becomes text, a link, a color or a measurement in one drag; anything on disk gets the small editor or converter it needs.

  • Capture Text from Screen and act on it: copy, open links, translate (macOS 15 or later) or read it aloud; scan QR codes and barcodes straight off the screen.
  • Pin a screen region so it floats above everything, keep captures on the Screen Shelf, measure a region, pick a color, or capture and mark up an image.
  • Clipboard History with search and pins; Text Editor with a file browser, code syntax and a Markdown preview that renders Mermaid diagrams.
  • Text Tools tidy whitespace, repair garbled characters, decode HTML, URLs and Base64, format JSON, redact contact details and convert to tables, on Apple's on-device models.
  • Image Tools and Batch Image Tools for markup, resize and export; PDF Quick Actions to combine, reorder, unlock and build PDFs from images; Large Text and Binary viewers for files other editors choke on.
Screen Tools use macOS Screen Recording only for the capture you start; color picking and measurement do not need it. Clipboard history and documents stay in local files you can inspect and delete.

Mac Health, Storage & Apps

Find out why the Mac is slow or full, then fix it with evidence on screen and nothing deleted behind your back.

  • Why Is My Mac Slow? gives a verdict with the contributing causes; Memory Health Monitor watches pressure and swap.
  • Startup & Background Inspector lists everything that runs at login, with signatures, and disables only your own items; Maintenance & System Tweaks shows dozens of macOS settings as cards, each with what is saved right now.
  • Reclaim Disk Space with guided actions and a storage map; Where Did My Space Go? draws your disk as a treemap; Redundancy & Clutter finds duplicates.
  • Uninstall an App with a reviewed file list, Find App Leftovers, and cleanup reports you can export; Install Apps & Dev Tools and Manage Fonts set a new Mac up.
  • Network Quality measures the connection you are on; Auto Wi-Fi Switch moves to a stronger saved network when the signal drops.
Cleanup moves items to the Trash and shows the list first. Locked, system-managed and important-to-review storage is never added to the total.

Private by Design

XHelpers Pro is built for local Mac workflows. It is not an analytics stream or a cloud file service, and its tools work signed out.

  • No account required for any tool; the Xin account is optional.
  • No analytics, advertising SDKs, usage telemetry or session tracking in the app.
  • Safe passwords, encryption keys, pointer profiles, remap rules, clipboard history, transcripts and audio processing stay on this Mac.
  • Nine optional permissions, each explained with what it unlocks and shown with its live status: Accessibility, Finder Extension, Full Disk Access, Location, Screen Recording, System Audio Recording, Speech Recognition, Display Resolution Helper, Automation.
  • Bring your own AI, or none: AI Settings picks OpenAI Codex, Anthropic Claude, xAI Grok, the OpenAI Responses API or Apple's on-device model for the features that can use one. Nothing leaves the Mac unless you connect a provider.
  • Feedback opens as an email draft you can read before sending.
