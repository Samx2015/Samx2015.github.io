THE XARIUM FLOW

Six steps from a fleeting idea to a useful record.

Every step is designed to keep the capture durable and the source visible.

  1. 01

    Share it

    Send a URL, selected text, image, PDF, screenshot, or file from the app where you found it.

  2. 02

    Stage it safely

    Xarium writes the received content to local staging first, even when the network is unavailable.

  3. 03

    See it in Inbox

    The new item appears right away with its source, capture time, type, and sync or processing state.

  4. 04

    Build useful layers

    After sync, extraction, OCR, normalization, and thumbnails make the item easier to search and review.

  5. 05

    Sync when ready

    Sign in with Apple to connect devices. Queued work retries and converges without making you capture twice.

  6. 06

    Use or take it with you

    Open the detail view, add notes or tags, export the workspace, or delete and restore an item when needed.

STATUS IN PLAIN ENGLISH

Know what is happening to every capture.

A status is information, not a scavenger hunt. Here is what the common states mean.

Stored locally

The original is safely on this device. Capture succeeded.

Waiting to sync

The item is queued and will upload when you are signed in and connected.

Processing pending

The capture is not lost. Server extraction, OCR, or thumbnail work has not finished yet.

Ready

Useful derived text or previews are available for review and search.

◎

Do I need to be logged in? No for local capture. Sign in with Apple when you want cloud sync, browser linking, and server-side processing across devices.
