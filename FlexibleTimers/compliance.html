Messaging Compliance

After a signed-in user gives explicit consent, XTimers by Xintech LLC may attempt to send a setup verification code to the user-entered proposed account phone number. After successful verification and opt-in, user-created timer/reminder SMS may be attempted only to that same phone number. Third-party recipient messaging is not enabled. SMS is not a two-way chat or conversation service.

SMS keywords: STOP opts out, HELP requests help, and START or YES may opt a previously opted-out phone number back in after app consent. These inbound keywords are used only for compliance and account-phone messaging controls. Carrier and provider handling may vary; XTimers does not promise a particular automated keyword response.
