XTimers by Xintech LLC SMS Terms

XTimers is operated by Xintech LLC. XTimers sends requested phone verification codes and user-scheduled SMS reminders to a signed-in user's own phone number. Reminder delivery requires a verified XTimers phone contact and a separate, active SMS reminder permission. XTimers does not send marketing messages, provide two-way SMS chat, or let users send SMS reminders to arbitrary third-party recipients.

Phone Verification and Reminder Consent

Xin Account sign-up requires an email address. A phone number is an optional XTimers contact; it does not create a phone-only Xin Account or become a Xin Account sign-in method. When you ask to verify your number, XTimers sends a one-time code for that request. Requesting or completing verification does not enable SMS reminders or reminder calls.

After verification, you can separately enable SMS reminders in the app. The permission control is not pre-selected. Before you agree, the app displays the reminder disclosure, these SMS Terms, and the Privacy Policy. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Call reminders have their own permission and Phone Reminder Terms. Enabling one reminder channel does not enable the other.

The English SMS reminder disclosure for policy version sms-reminders-2026-09-13 is:

I agree to receive automated SMS reminder messages from XTimers by Xintech LLC for reminders I schedule for myself at this phone number. Message frequency varies. Standard message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop phone reminders and HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. XTimers does not send marketing messages.

The app may show a localized disclosure. XTimers records the exact disclosure you accepted and its policy version. An existing valid SMS reminder grant remains valid for the same verified contact; it never authorizes calls. See the phone verification and consent flow.

Message Frequency and Availability

Message frequency varies with your verification requests and the SMS reminders you schedule for yourself. Delivery depends on service and carrier availability and the SMS destinations supported by XTimers. SMS destination support is separate from the USA-and-Canada restriction on reminder calls. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Standard message and data rates may apply and are your responsibility.

Turn Off or Remove Phone Reminders

Turning off SMS reminders in the app revokes only SMS reminder permission. Your verified contact and separate call reminder choice stay in place. Removing or replacing the verified phone contact revokes both reminder permissions and makes reminders tied to the old contact ineligible for delivery. Starting a replacement or canceling verification keeps your existing contact and permissions until the new number is successfully verified and the replacement is confirmed.

Re-adding a number or enabling a permission again does not restore canceled or missed reminders. Schedule a new reminder if you want another delivery. A message already submitted for delivery may still arrive after you turn a permission off or remove the contact.

STOP, START, and HELP

Reply STOP to an XTimers SMS to stop automated phone reminders. XTimers treats this external opt-out as applying to both SMS reminders and reminder calls. Pressing 9 during an XTimers reminder call has the same effect. The carrier or messaging provider may also block verification codes after an SMS STOP. Removing and re-adding your number does not erase a provider block or your opt-out history.

For the XTimers toll-free SMS number, reply START or UNSTOP to request that the messaging provider remove its SMS block. A provider confirmation does not grant XTimers SMS or call reminder permission. Return to the app and explicitly review and enable each reminder permission you want. Previously canceled or missed reminders remain canceled or missed. If delivery remains blocked, contact support.

Reply HELP for support information, or visit XTimers Support. Inbound messages are used for these service controls; XTimers does not accept general SMS conversations.

Privacy

XTimers retains verification, consent, revocation, and delivery records as described in the Privacy Policy. Xintech LLC does not sell or share phone numbers or SMS opt-in information for third-party marketing or promotional purposes.
