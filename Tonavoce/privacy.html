Tonavoce Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-03 · Web copy: https://xintechllc.com/Tonavoce/privacy.html

Scope and controller

This policy explains how Tonavoce handles information on your iPhone. Xintech LLC is the controller. Tonavoce is an app and a keyboard that turn your speech into text with a speech model that runs on your iPhone.

Tonavoce has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. Xintech LLC receives nothing from it, and nothing is sold or shared.

Your voice

  • Tonavoce uses the microphone only while a dictation session runs. You start a session; it ends on its own after a few minutes without dictation, or when you stop it. While it runs, iOS shows the orange microphone indicator.
  • During a session the microphone stays open in the background so the keyboard can dictate, but sound is kept only between your tap on the keyboard's mic and your tap on Stop.
  • That sound is held in memory only, never written to storage, and discarded as soon as it is transcribed or cancelled. If Tonavoce stops, the sound is lost.
  • Transcription runs entirely on your iPhone. Your voice is never sent anywhere.

Your text

  • The transcribed text is passed from the app to its keyboard through a small file in a storage area that only Tonavoce and its keyboard can read. The file is protected by your device passcode and excluded from device backups.
  • The file is deleted as soon as the keyboard inserts the text or you dismiss or cancel it, and at most 2 minutes after the text was ready. If neither the app nor the keyboard runs at that moment, the file is deleted the next time either one starts.
  • Tonavoce does not keep a history of what you dictated, and it never writes your words to its logs.

The keyboard and Full Access

To dictate, the Tonavoce keyboard needs Full Access. iOS warns that a keyboard with Full Access could send what you type. Tonavoce's keyboard sends nothing over the network: Full Access is only how it talks to the Tonavoce app on the same iPhone, which holds the microphone that iOS does not give keyboards. Without Full Access the keyboard still types, but cannot dictate. The keyboard does not record or keep what you type.

Network

Tonavoce connects to the internet only when you tap Download or Repair to get the speech model, about 632 MB, from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Those requests carry no information about you or what you dictate. Hugging Face's own privacy policy applies to its servers.

What stays on your iPhone

  • the speech model, until you delete it in Tonavoce or delete the app; it is excluded from backups because it can be downloaded again;
  • your setting for how long an idle session lasts;
  • the time the keyboard last ran with Full Access, so setup can show that step as done.

Deleting Tonavoce removes all of these.

Children

Tonavoce collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
