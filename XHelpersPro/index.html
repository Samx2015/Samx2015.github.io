Display & Windows

Your displays and windows arranged the way you work, without a second utility.

  • Work on the big monitor alone: black out the MacBook’s built-in screen while it mirrors, and dim every display until you touch a key.
  • Five switches, one menu: brightness, contrast, keyboard backlight and the macOS theme, without hunting through System Settings.
  • Get crisp text at the size you want on any external display: HiDPI presets and custom resolutions, with a safety timer that reverts a bad switch on its own.
  • Put every window where it belongs with one shortcut: halves, quadrants you design, center, maximize or the next display, and bring a whole layout back after unplugging.
  • Never lose the active window on a wide desk, and keep the Mac awake exactly as long as a download or a talk needs.

Screen, Text & Files

Anything on screen becomes text, a link, a color or a measurement in one drag; anything on disk gets the small editor or converter it needs.

  • Stop retyping what is on screen: drag over any text, then copy it, open its links, translate it (macOS 15 or later) or hear it read aloud; QR codes and barcodes scan the same way.
  • Keep a reference in view while you work: pin a region above every window, park captures on the Screen Shelf, measure or pick a color, or mark up a capture and send it.
  • Never lose something you copied: searchable Clipboard History with pins, and a Text Editor with a file browser, code syntax and a Markdown preview that renders Mermaid diagrams.
  • Fix messy text without a script: tidy whitespace, repair garbled characters, decode HTML, URLs and Base64, format JSON, redact contact details or turn lines into a table, all on Apple’s on-device models.
  • Handle images and PDFs without opening five apps: resize, mark up and export in batches; combine, reorder, unlock or build PDFs; and open huge text or binary files other editors choke on.

AI Usage

Every AI subscription’s remaining quota in one place, before you hit the wall.

  • See every account at a glance: weekly quota remaining, the next reset and the limit resets still available, for each connected Codex and Claude account side by side.
  • Check without opening a window: hover the AI meter in the menu for a card of every account, or pin the one you use most to the menu bar.
  • Eleven services, one dashboard: Codex, Claude, Grok, the OpenAI, Anthropic and xAI APIs, Gemini, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, OpenRouter and DeepSeek.
  • Readings stay yours: usage is collected on this Mac through the bundled CodexBar, and every reading keeps its source timestamp.

Finder, Safes & Archives

The file jobs you do ten times a day, done from the right-click menu you already use.

  • Skip the detours: create a file or folder where you are, copy a path, paste or move a selection, and open anything in any of 40+ apps, from Terminal and VS Code to Codex and Claude.
  • Lock private files in seconds: a selection becomes an encrypted Safe, 7z, ZIP or DMG from one dialog, with a password only you hold.
  • Keep a Safe growing without repacking it, and take out only the items you need.
  • Open any archive someone sends you, zip to rar and password-protected ones included, without installing another unarchiver.
  • Read Markdown with a spacebar tap in Finder, and start a clean uninstall from the same right-click menu.

Pointer & Keyboard

Make every mouse, trackpad and keyboard feel exactly right, and keep it that way.

  • Aim like you mean it: sensitivity and acceleration tuned per device, with acceleration off entirely for raw, predictable movement.
  • Scroll the way each device should, with speed, acceleration and direction set per mouse or trackpad, so a wheel and a trackpad stop fighting each other.
  • Turn a spare key or mouse button into the shortcut you reach for most, make Caps Lock do two jobs, or add a Hyper key that never clashes with an app.
  • Run the whole suite from the keyboard: 67 actions ready to bind in one Global Shortcuts window.

Audio, Music & Subtitles

Control what each app sounds like, keep every source in one place, and read what any app is saying.

  • Stop reaching for the volume every time a call starts: give each app its own level and mute, boost quiet apps up to 200%, and shape them with per-app Audio Effects.
  • Mixed playlists in one library: combine Apple Music, music from YouTube, and local audio and video in the same playlist, with saved filters across your library.
  • A lightweight player for the file you just downloaded: a shared queue, Quick Video with speed, loop and always-on-top, and the audio pulled out of any video in a click.
  • Follow any meeting, stream or video in your language: Live Subtitles transcribe and translate one app’s audio on device, with a captions-only mode and per-speaker colors (macOS 26.4 or later).

Mac Health, Storage & Apps

Find out why the Mac is slow or full, then fix it with evidence on screen and nothing deleted behind your back.

  • Get a straight answer to “why is my Mac slow?”: a verdict with the causes behind it, and a Memory Health Monitor that watches pressure and swap before things grind.
  • See everything that starts with your Mac, with signatures, and switch off only what is yours; browse dozens of macOS settings as cards that show what is saved right now.
  • Win back space with confidence: guided reclaim actions and a storage map, a treemap of where the disk went, and duplicates found by Redundancy & Clutter.
  • Remove an app properly: a reviewed file list before anything moves, leftovers found for apps you deleted long ago, and cleanup reports you can export; set a new Mac up with Install Apps & Dev Tools and Manage Fonts.
  • Know whether it is the Wi-Fi or the site: Network Quality measures the connection you are on, and Auto Wi-Fi Switch moves to a stronger saved network when the signal drops.
