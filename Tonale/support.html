Tonale Support

Tonale plays the music and videos on your Mac, your Apple Music library, and YouTube videos, and puts them all in playlists you build.

Requires macOS 14 or later.

Write to admin@xintechllc.com with anything this page does not answer. Tell us your macOS version and your Tonale version (Tonale › About Tonale).

Your Files

How do I add my own music and videos?

Choose File › Add Folder…, or use Add Local Audio… or Add Local Video… in the library. You can also drag files into the library. Tonale plays the audio and video formats macOS supports.

Why does Tonale ask me to choose files or folders?

Tonale runs in Apple's App Sandbox, so it can open only what you give it. When you choose a file or folder, macOS lets Tonale open it, and Tonale remembers it. Tonale does not look anywhere else on your disk.

An item says "Unavailable". What happened?

The file moved, was renamed, is on a disk that is not connected, or macOS no longer lets Tonale open it. Use Locate… or Repair… on the item and choose the file again. Other files in the same folder reconnect with it. If a whole folder is affected, use Reauthorize….

Does Tonale copy or change my files?

No. Tonale plays your files where they are. It does not copy, move, change, or delete them.

Apple Music

Do I need an Apple Music subscription?

Yes, to play Apple Music content. Your own files and YouTube videos work without one. Xintech LLC does not sell Apple Music.

Tonale does not show my Apple Music library.

Tonale needs permission to use your media library. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Media & Apple Music, turn on Tonale, and open Tonale again. If the library is still empty, sign in to Apple Music in the Music app first.

Some songs do not play.

Protected songs play only through Apple Music. If a song is not available in your country or region, or has left the Apple Music catalog, Tonale cannot play it.

YouTube

Can Tonale play YouTube audio in the background?

No. Tonale uses YouTube's official embedded player. Google's rules for that player pause the video when the player is hidden, minimized, mostly covered, or off the screen, or when the display sleeps. Keep the player visible while a YouTube item plays.

Can Tonale download a video or save its audio?

No. Tonale does not download videos, save their audio, or block ads.

Do I need a Google account?

No. You can add, play, and build playlists from public videos and playlists without signing in. You cannot connect a YouTube account in this version.

Tonale asks "Use the public YouTube service?"

To show titles and the contents of public playlists, Tonale asks a Xintech LLC service for public YouTube details. Tonale asks once before it uses the service. The Privacy Policy says what the service receives.

Can I play YouTube in Chrome instead?

In the Tonale you download from this website, yes. In Settings › YouTube, set Play YouTube videos to In Chrome. Then use Choose Chrome folder… and Get Chrome extension. To stop, set it back to In Tonale and remove the extension in Chrome.

Accounts and Sync

Do I need an account?

No. Tonale works fully without one. An account keeps your library the same on your Macs.

How do I sign in?

Choose Tonale › Settings…, then Account & Sync. Choose Continue with Google or Continue with Apple, or enter your email address and sign in with an emailed code or your password. If you do not have a Xin Account yet, the emailed code creates one, or you can use Create a Xin Account. Your Xin Account is the same account that other Xin apps use. When you sign in once on a Mac, Xin apps that share sign-in, such as XHelpers Pro, can use the same sign-in there. Other Xin apps, such as XTimers, have their own sign-in.

I forgot my password.

Choose Email code and sign in with a code instead. Or choose Password, then Forgot password?, and choose Reset. Tonale emails you a link; open it on the same Mac to set a new password.

I signed in, and my library looks different.

While you are signed in, Tonale shows your synced library. The library you used before signing in is still there, under On This Mac in the sidebar. It is read-only while you are signed in. Use Copy to Synced Library, or Copy All to Synced Library, to add its playlists to your synced library. Hide On This Mac hides the section. Nothing on this Mac is deleted.

What syncs?

The playlists and saved filters you made, the order of their entries, and references to the items in them. Your files, file paths, passwords, and device identifiers never sync, and no settings sync in this version. Lists that Tonale builds for you from a source, such as your Apple Music library, are built again on each Mac.

My playlist is on my other Mac, but its files say "Unavailable".

Tonale syncs the playlist, not the files, and never sends file paths. If the other Mac has the same file in Local Audio or Local Video, Tonale finds it by its fingerprint. Otherwise, locate the file once there and the items reconnect.

Two Macs changed the same item. What happens?

Account & Sync shows the conflict and lets you choose Keep server version or Keep my version. Tonale does not silently discard an edit.

What happens when I sign out?

Tonale ends the session on this Mac, and Xin apps that share sign-in, such as XHelpers Pro, are signed out on this Mac too. Nothing on the server is deleted. Your synced library stays on this Mac, hidden, until you sign in to the same account again.

Deleting Your Data

In Tonale, open Settings › Account & Sync and use the Account deletion menu. Each choice asks you to type DELETE and to sign in again, and each is permanent.

  • Delete Tonale Data… deletes your synced library on the server and your account's library on this Mac. Your Xin Account and your other Xin apps stay.
  • Delete Xin Account… (when it is offered) deletes your Xin Account and its data in all your Xin apps.
  • Delete Unfinished Xin Signup… is for when you signed in but did not finish creating a Xin Account.

Your media files are never touched. To remove Tonale from your Mac, quit it and move it to the Trash. Its library stays in ~/Library/Containers/com.xintechllc.tonale until you delete that folder.

To ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of information that Xintech LLC holds about you, write to admin@xintechllc.com.

Using Tonale with XHelpers Pro

How do XHelpers Pro and Tonale work together?

In XHelpers Pro, Music & Playlists (Tonale)… opens Tonale. Both use the same installed Tonale, with one library and one set of settings. XHelpers Pro can also save its queue as a Tonale playlist with Save Queue as Playlist….

XHelpers Pro says Tonale is not installed.

Choose Install & Open, or Download Tonale to get it from this website. After you install it, XHelpers Pro opens the installed Tonale.

An imported item says "Unavailable".

Tonale can open only the files that XHelpers Pro opened with it. Locate any other file once in Tonale.

Updates

How do I get updates?

The Tonale you download from this website updates itself. Choose Tonale › Check for Updates… to check now.

Tonale says "This library was updated by a newer Tonale."

Update Tonale to open the library. Tonale cannot open a library that a newer version changed. Do not delete the library to make an older Tonale open it.

Other Questions

Does Tonale collect analytics?

No. Tonale has no analytics, no advertising, and no crash-reporting SDK, and it sends no background usage data. See the Privacy Policy.

Which languages does Tonale support?

English and 25 other languages. Tonale uses the language you choose in macOS.
